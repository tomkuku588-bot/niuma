牛马脱敏器隐私政策

更新日期：2026-11-07 | 生效日期：2026-11-07

欢迎使用牛马脱敏器（Niuma）。本政策适用于牛马脱敏器 HarmonyOS 版本在安装、使用等过程中对信息的处理方式。开发者/运营者为本应用的发布主体，具体以华为应用市场页面公示的实名认证主体为准；联系邮箱为 tomkuku588@gmail.com。本应用是一款完全离线运行的薪资估算工具，免费、无广告、无需注册。

1. 我们处理的信息范围及用途

我们处理的信息主要限于你主动设置或在使用过程中在本地生成的数据，包括薪资模式与金额、月计薪天数、上下班与午休时间、工作日选择、调休记录、加班倍率、发薪日、货币符号、心愿清单、语录、显示精度、主题、防窥开关等应用设置，以及基于这些设置在本机生成的今日估算、下班倒计时、心愿进度和桌面卡片快照。处理这些信息的目的在于实现薪资与作息的本地估算、页面与桌面卡片展示、备份迁移及必要的故障排查。当前版本不保存每日收入流水或考勤记录，不提供账号注册或登录，也不默认收集手机号、电子邮箱、精准位置信息、通讯录、短信、通话记录、相册、人脸、指纹等个人信息。

2. 信息存储位置、期限与删除

上述信息默认存储在你的设备本地应用沙箱内：应用设置保存在 preferences（niuma.cfg）中，桌面卡片使用沙箱文件 settings.snapshot.json 读取快照，卡片标识保存在 widget-forms.json 中。上述数据不会上传至开发者服务器，当前版本不提供云同步。信息保存期限取决于你的使用行为；当你修改或删除相应设置、清除应用数据、卸载应用或重置设备后，相应数据将被删除。若你将备份或导出文件另行复制到其他设备、云盘或第三方服务，相关存储行为由你自行决定并负责。

3. 权限与系统能力说明

当前版本的核心功能在离线状态下即可完整运行，不以联网为前提。当前版本未声明任何系统权限。为实现备份迁移，应用会调用 HarmonyOS 系统文件选择器（DocumentViewPicker）读取或保存 JSON 文件，范围由你在系统选择器中选定；当你点击复制或分享时，应用会把生成的文本写入系统剪贴板。桌面卡片扩展仅读取本地快照文件用于展示。除实现前述功能所必需的系统能力外，当前版本不以后台方式读取无关文件。若后续版本新增权限或系统能力，我们会在相应功能触发前另行说明，并依法取得相应授权。

4. 信息共享、转让与公开披露

我们不会将你的个人信息出售、出租、交换或无关共享给第三方，也不会擅自公开披露。仅在以下情形下可能发生信息对外流转：你主动使用复制、分享、导出或备份迁移等能力；法律法规、监管机关或司法机关要求；或者为保护你、其他用户或公众的人身财产安全所必需。在上述情形下，我们会在法律允许的范围内采取合理保护措施。

5. 第三方 SDK 与对外服务

除 HarmonyOS 系统基础能力外，当前版本不接入广告、商业化统计分析、第三方登录、地图、支付、推送等第三方 SDK；应用的依赖为空对象，不向开发者自建服务器或任何外部服务上传你的薪资、作息、心愿、语录、调休等数据，也不接入任何 AI 或云端计算服务。若后续版本引入第三方服务，我们会同步更新本政策，并在必要时通过弹窗、页面提示或系统授权流程取得你的明示同意。

6. 信息安全

我们会采取与当前产品形态相适应的合理安全措施，包括本地化处理、最小必要原则和限制无关数据访问等，以降低信息被未经授权访问、披露、篡改或丢失的风险。当前版本的应用设置与备份文件以明文形式保存在本机，未做加密处理；请你妥善保管自己的设备、系统账号以及自行导出的文件。任何技术措施都无法保证绝对安全。

Niuma Privacy Policy

Updated on 2026-11-07 | Effective on 2026-11-07

Welcome to Niuma (牛马脱敏器). This policy applies to the HarmonyOS version of the app and explains how information is handled when you install and use it. The developer/operator is the entity publishing the app, as displayed on the AppGallery listing under the verified developer identity. The contact email is tomkuku588@gmail.com. The app is a fully offline salary estimation tool that is free, ad-free, and requires no registration.

1. What Information We Process and Why

We process mainly the data you actively configure or that is generated locally while you use the app, including app settings such as salary mode and amount, monthly paid days, start/end and lunch times, selected workdays, day overrides, overtime rate, payday, currency symbol, wish list, quotes, decimal precision, theme, and privacy masking, as well as the daily estimate, off-work countdown, wish progress, and home-screen card snapshot generated locally from those settings. The purposes are local salary and schedule estimation, page and home-screen card display, backup migration, and necessary troubleshooting. The current version does not keep a daily income ledger or attendance records, does not provide account registration or login, and does not collect your phone number, email address, precise location, contacts, SMS, call logs, photo library, facial data, fingerprints, or similar personal information by default.

2. Where Information Is Stored, How Long, and How to Delete It

The above information is stored by default in the app sandbox on your device: app settings are kept in preferences (niuma.cfg), the home-screen card reads a snapshot from the sandbox file settings.snapshot.json, and card identifiers are kept in widget-forms.json. This data is not uploaded to any developer-operated server, and the current version does not provide cloud sync. Retention depends on your actions. When you modify or delete the relevant settings, clear app data, uninstall the app, or reset the device, the corresponding data will be deleted. If you copy backup or exported files to another device, cloud drive, or third-party service, that storage is controlled by you.

3. Permissions and System Capabilities

The core features of the current version run fully offline and do not depend on network access. The current version declares no system permissions. For backup migration, the app invokes the HarmonyOS system document picker (DocumentViewPicker) to read or save a JSON file, with the scope selected by you in the system picker. When you tap copy or share, the app writes the generated text to the system clipboard. The home-screen card extension only reads the local snapshot file for display. Other than the system capabilities necessary for those functions, the current version does not read unrelated files in the background. If a future version adds new permissions or system capabilities, we will explain them before the relevant feature is triggered and obtain authorization where required by law.

4. Sharing, Transfer, and Public Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal information with unrelated third parties, and we do not publicly disclose it without authorization. External transfer may occur only when you actively use copy, share, export, or backup migration features; when required by laws, regulators, or courts; or when necessary to protect the personal or property safety of you, other users, or the public. In such cases, we will take reasonable protective measures within the scope permitted by law.

5. Third-Party SDKs and External Services

Except for HarmonyOS base system capabilities, the current version does not integrate third-party SDKs for advertising, commercial analytics, social login, maps, payment, or push notifications. The app's dependencies object is empty. It does not upload your salary, schedule, wishes, quotes, day overrides, or other data to any developer-operated server or external service, and it does not use any AI or cloud computing service. If a future version introduces third-party services, we will update this policy and, where necessary, obtain your explicit consent through notices, dialogs, or system authorization flows.

6. Data Security

We adopt reasonable safeguards appropriate to the current product form, including local processing, the minimum-necessary principle, and limiting unnecessary data access, in order to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. In the current version, app settings and backup files are stored on-device in plaintext without encryption. Please keep your device, system accounts, and exported files secure. No technical measure can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

You can view, modify, and delete salary and schedule settings, wishes, quotes, and day overrides in the app, or uninstall the app to delete all local data. Because the current version does not provide an online account system, access, correction, and deletion can be performed locally. If you have questions about this policy, data handling, or permission use, you may contact us through the email provided in this policy.

8. Minors

If you are under the age of 14, you should use the app only after your parent or legal guardian has read and agreed to this policy. We do not knowingly conduct targeted marketing toward minors or intentionally collect personal information from minors that is unrelated to the app's functions.

9. Policy Updates and Contact

If there is a material change to product features, processing purposes, data types, permission use, or applicable rules, we will update this policy and provide notice in the app or on a prominent page. You may contact us at tomkuku588@gmail.com, or visit the GitHub repository at https://github.com/tomkuku588-bot/niuma.
